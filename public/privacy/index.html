Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

Social via Email is a free, open-source, client-side web application.

Information Collected by the Developer

Social via Email is purely client-side (React app running in your browser). The developer does not operate an app backend server and does not collect, store, or receive your personal data.

  • We do not store your email address, contacts, chats, or conversations.
  • The app uses short-lived OAuth 2.0 access tokens from Google. These tokens are held only in browser memory and expire after 1 hour (or when you sign out/refresh).
  • No cookies, no localStorage, no persistent storage is used for user data.

Google Data We Access and Why

When you sign in with Gmail, the app requests Google OAuth access for openid, email, profile, and Gmail modify permissions. The app uses Google user data only to provide the visible Social via Email features:

  • Profile information (email address, display name, and profile picture) is used to identify you in the app and as your Social via Email identity.
  • Gmail labels are created or found to organize app data and app-related messages, including social-via-email-data, social-via-email-inbox, and social-via-email-ai.
  • Gmail messages with app-related labels or the Lemitar::Social-via-Email subject are read so the app can restore saved state, scan incoming Social via Email messages, and process contacts, chats, and conversations.
  • Gmail send access is used to send contact requests, chat messages, and conversation posts as emails from your Gmail account to the recipient email addresses you choose.
  • Gmail modify access is used to create app state emails, apply labels, move processed messages to Trash, and keep app-related sent messages organized.
  • Your data is processed entirely in your browser and in your Gmail account.
  • We (the developer) never see or receive your Google data.

The use and transfer of information received from Google Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing and Transfer

The developer does not receive, sell, rent, or transfer your Google user data. The app transfers data only when needed for user-facing features that you initiate:

  • When you add contacts, send chats, or post conversations, the app sends email messages from your Gmail account to the recipient email addresses involved.
  • Those emails contain structured JSON data needed for the feature, such as sender and recipient email addresses, contact information, subscriber lists, and message content.
  • Recipients and their email providers receive those messages the same way they receive ordinary email.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, retargeting, personalized ads, data brokerage, credit-worthiness, lending purposes, or training generalized AI or machine learning models.

Retention and Deletion

Social via Email does not retain Google user data on developer-controlled systems. Data retention depends on your browser session and your Gmail account:

  • OAuth access tokens and in-memory app state are cleared when you refresh or close the tab, sign out, or when the short-lived access token expires.
  • App-created Gmail labels remain in your Gmail account until you delete them.
  • If you choose to save app state, the app stores contacts, chats, and conversations as a memory-dump email in your Gmail account under the social-via-email-data label.
  • When the app replaces old saved state, scans processed incoming app messages, or cleans up app-related sent messages, it moves those messages to Gmail Trash. The app does not permanently delete messages by bypassing Trash.
  • You can delete app-created labels and messages directly in Gmail at any time.
  • You can revoke the app's Google access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Data Protection and Security

Social via Email protects Google user data using the following mechanisms:

  • The app runs entirely in your browser and does not operate a backend server or developer-controlled database.
  • Google OAuth access tokens are held only in browser memory. They are not stored in cookies, localStorage, IndexedDB, or any server-side storage.
  • The app does not request or store Google refresh tokens. Access tokens are short-lived and expire automatically.
  • Communication with Google APIs occurs over HTTPS.
  • Google user data processed by the app remains in browser memory during the active session, except for app-created emails and labels that remain in your Gmail account under your control.
  • Saved app state, if you choose to save it, is stored as an email in your own Gmail account and protected by your Google account security.
  • The developer does not receive, view, store, sell, or transfer your Google user data.
