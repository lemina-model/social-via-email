Use of Google Account Data

When you sign in with Gmail, the app requests OAuth 2.0 access to your Gmail account solely to provide its social features (contacts, chats, and conversations). Specifically:

  • Access tokens are short-lived (1 hour) and held only in browser memory.
  • No Google account data is transmitted to or stored by the developer.
  • You may revoke access at any time via your Google Account permissions page.

Use of Google API data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
